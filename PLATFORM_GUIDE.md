## What is Beam Affiliate?

Beam Affiliate is an **affiliate marketing platform**. It allows your business to run a referral/partner programme where:

- **Partners (Affiliates)** share a unique link or referral code with potential customers.
- When a customer signs up or makes a purchase through that link, the partner earns a **commission**.
- The platform tracks everything — clicks, sign-ups, sales, and commissions — automatically.
- Admins can approve commissions, process payouts, and manage all partners from a single dashboard.

Think of it like having your own in-house version of a referral network, fully owned and controlled by your team.

---

## The Big Picture

Here is the complete flow in plain English:

```
[Partner shares referral link]
         ↓
[Customer clicks the link — click is recorded]
         ↓
[Customer signs up or makes a purchase]
         ↓
[Platform automatically creates a "Conversion"]
         ↓
[A Commission is calculated based on the commission rate]
         ↓
[Commission enters a "Hold Period" (e.g. 30 days for refunds)]
         ↓
[Admin approves the commission]
         ↓
[Admin creates a Payout for the partner]
         ↓
[Partner receives payment via Bank Transfer or Beam Wallet]
```

Every step above is tracked, logged, and visible in the Admin Dashboard.

## The Two Dashboards

The platform has two completely separate areas:

### 🔴 Admin Dashboard — `/admin`
For your internal team only. Admins can:
- See all partners and their performance
- Approve or reject commissions
- Create and process payouts
- Manage partner groups and commission rates
- View analytics and reports
- Manage program settings (your brand, payout rules, etc.)
- Manage the team (invite other admins)

### 🟢 Affiliate / Partner Dashboard — `/affiliate`
For your partners (affiliates). They can:
- See their referral link and unique code
- Track their clicks, referrals, and earnings
- View the status of their commissions
- Request or see their payout history
- Download reports
- Access marketing resources you upload for them

## The Affiliate Journey

This is what a partner experiences from start to finish:

### Step 1 — Registration
A partner visits `/register` and creates an account with their name, email, and Beam Wallet number. They receive a 6-digit OTP (one-time code) via email to verify their identity.

### Step 2 — Getting Their Link
After logging in, they land on their **Dashboard** at `/affiliate`. They immediately see:
- Their unique **referral link** (e.g. `https://yoursite.com?ref=ABCD1234`)
- Their **referral code** (the short code they can share manually)

### Step 3 — Sharing & Earning
The partner shares their link across social media, email newsletters, WhatsApp, etc. Every time someone clicks their link, it is recorded.

### Step 4 — Tracking Results
Inside their dashboard, partners see:
- **Referrals** — people they have sent to your business
- **Conversions** — referrals who became paying customers
- **Commissions** — money they have earned (shown as Pending → Approved → Paid)
- **Transactions** — detailed record of every earning event

### Step 5 — Getting Paid
Once commissions are approved and the hold period passes, the Admin processes a payout. The partner can see their payout history under the **Payouts** section.

## The Admin Journey

This is what your internal team does to keep the programme running:

### Step 1 — Overview Dashboard (`/admin`)
The home page shows at a glance:
- Total revenue generated by affiliates
- Number of active partners
- Commissions pending approval
- Recent activity feed

### Step 2 — Managing Partners (`/admin/partners`)
- View all registered affiliates with their stats (clicks, conversions, earnings)
- Approve or suspend a partner's account
- Set a custom commission rate for individual partners (override the default)
- View a detailed profile page for each partner (`/admin/partners/[id]`)

### Step 3 — Approving Commissions (`/admin` → Commissions section)
Commissions come in as **Pending** after a conversion. The team reviews and either:
- ✅ **Approves** — moves commission to the approved queue, eligible for payout
- ❌ **Rejects / Clawback** — if the sale was fraudulent or refunded

### Step 4 — Processing Payouts (`/admin/payouts`)
Once commissions are approved:
1. Select the partner
2. Select the approved commissions to pay out
3. Choose the payout method (Bank Transfer or Beam Wallet)
4. Click "Process Payout"
5. The partner receives an email notification automatically

### Step 5 — Program Settings (`/admin/program-settings`)
Configure the entire programme:
- Your company name, logo, brand colours
- Default commission rate (e.g. 20%)
- Minimum payout threshold (e.g. partners must earn at least €100 before they can be paid)
- Commission hold period (how many days before a commission can be paid out)
- Payout frequency (monthly, weekly, etc.)

### Step 6 — Partner Groups (`/admin/programs`)
You can organise partners into groups with different commission rates. For example:
- **Bronze Partners** — 15% commission
- **Silver Partners** — 20% commission
- **Gold Partners** — 25% commission

Assigning a partner to a group automatically gives them that group's rate.

## Key Concepts Explained Simply

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Affiliate / Partner** | A person or business who refers customers to you in exchange for a commission |
| **Referral Link** | A unique URL that identifies which partner sent a customer |
| **Referral Code** | A short code (e.g. `BEAM2024`) that partners can share; works the same as the link |
| **Conversion** | A referral who completed a desired action (e.g. signed up, made a purchase) |
| **Commission** | The amount of money the partner earns from a conversion |
| **Commission Rate** | The percentage of a sale value that goes to the partner (e.g. 20% of €100 = €20) |
| **Hold Period** | A waiting period (e.g. 30 days) after a conversion before a commission can be paid out — protects against refunds |
| **Payout** | The actual payment sent to the partner for their approved commissions |
| **Partner Group** | A category (e.g. Gold, Silver) with a set commission rate that multiple partners can share |
| **Reseller ID** | A unique internal ID assigned to each partner for accounting purposes |
| **OTP** | One-Time Password — a 6-digit code sent to email to verify identity during login/registration |
| **Audit Log** | A behind-the-scenes record of every action taken in the system (who did what and when) |

## Payouts — How Partners Get Paid

The platform supports two payout methods:

### 🏦 Bank Transfer
The partner provides their bank details (IBAN, BIC etc.) in their profile under **Settings → Payout Details**. The admin downloads a bank file or manually initiates the transfer.

### 💳 Beam Wallet
If the partner has a Beam Wallet number, payouts can be sent directly to their Beam Wallet. The partner provides their Beam number during registration or in their profile settings.

### The Payout Flow
```
Admin selects partner + approved commissions
         ↓
Admin clicks "Process Payout"
         ↓
Payout record is created with status: PENDING
         ↓
Admin confirms payment was sent externally
         ↓
Admin marks payout as COMPLETED
         ↓
Partner receives "Payment Sent" email automatically
```

> ⚠️ **Important:** The platform creates a payout record and sends emails, but the actual money movement (bank transfer) is done manually or via Beam Wallet's API. The platform is the management layer, not the payment gateway itself.

## Environment & Setup

> This section is for reference. Your technical team handles these. If something stops working, here is what to point them to.

The platform requires the following external services to be configured:

| Service | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **PostgreSQL Database** (Neon) | Stores all data — partners, commissions, payouts, etc. |
| **Resend** | Sends all automated emails |
| **Stripe** | Handles card payments on the checkout page |
| **Beam Wallet API** | Enables Beam Wallet payouts |
| **Vercel** | Hosts the website and all API endpoints |

If the team reports that emails aren't being received, the first place to check is the Resend API key and the email logs at `/admin/emails`.

If payouts via Beam Wallet aren't working, the Beam Wallet API key needs to be verified.

## 🚦 Status Definitions Quick Reference

### Commission Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PENDING` | Commission created, within the hold period — not yet payable |
| `APPROVED` | Hold period passed, approved by admin — ready for payout |
| `PAID` | Included in a payout that has been processed |
| `CANCELLED` | Rejected or reversed |
| `CLAWBACK` | Commission was reversed (e.g. customer refunded) |

### Payout Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PENDING` | Payout created but payment not yet sent |
| `PROCESSING` | Payment is being processed |
| `COMPLETED` | Payment successfully sent to partner |
| `FAILED` | Payment attempt failed |

### Affiliate / User Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PENDING` | Newly registered, waiting for admin approval |
| `ACTIVE` | Approved and can use the platform |
| `INACTIVE` | Temporarily deactivated |
| `SUSPENDED` | Suspended due to policy violation |

## 💬 Common Questions

**Q: A partner says they can't log in. What do I do?**  
A: Check their account status in `/admin/partners`. If it shows `PENDING` or `SUSPENDED`, you can change their status to `ACTIVE` from their profile page.

**Q: A partner says their referral link isn't working. What do I do?**  
A: Ask your technical team to check if their referral code is correctly set up in the database. They can check `/admin/partners/[id]` for the partner's referral code.

**Q: How do I add a new admin user?**  
A: Go to `/admin/team` and invite them via email. They'll receive an invitation link to register as an admin.

**Q: How do I change the commission rate for everyone?**  
A: Go to `/admin/program-settings` and update the default commission rate. This applies to all partners who don't have an individual override or a group rate.

**Q: How do I give one partner a special higher commission rate?**  
A: Go to `/admin/partners/[id]` for that specific partner, and set a "Commission Rate Override" for them. This takes priority over everything else.

**Q: A partner completed a referral but no commission appeared. Why?**  
A: A commission is only created when a tracked **Conversion** occurs. If the customer didn't click the referral link before signing up (e.g. they typed the URL directly), no commission is created. Conversions must go through the tracked referral link.
